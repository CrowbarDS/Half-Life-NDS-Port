# What each row does, for the box under the list.
HelpRoomEffectsDsp = The rooms heard: off, as the game has them, or richer from the DSi's sound processor.
HelpRoomEffects = The rooms heard -- a tunnel, a chamber, under water -- as the game has them, or off.
HelpOutput = Speakers: a little narrower, for the console's own. Headphones: all of it. Mono: one.
HelpQualityDsi = High: the sound made at the rate the DSi's speakers play it, 47.6 kHz. A little more work.
HelpQualityDs = On a DSi: the sound made at the rate its speakers play it.
HelpMusic = How loud the music is.
HelpEffects = How loud the guns, the blows and the world are.
HelpVoices = How loud the people and the monsters speak.
HelpAmbience = How loud the hum and drip of a place is.
HelpFocus = While someone speaks, the music and the hum of a place are let down: off, a little, or more.
HelpFog = Fog, where the view ends: off, only where the map is too big to draw whole, or always.
HelpFogStrength = How thick the fog is.
HelpFogDistance = How near the fog begins.
HelpFogColour = The colour the far distance fades into.
HelpModelDetail = How much of the people and monsters is drawn.
HelpWorldDetail = How much of the walls is drawn far off.
HelpModelLimit = How many people and monsters are drawn at once: auto, or a number.
HelpDrawDistance = How far the world is drawn.
HelpDsiLight = On a DSi: the light on the walls drawn finer, its pools and shadows as the game has them. A little more work.
HelpScreen = The top screen's shape. 16:10 is for a 3DS whose screen TWPatch made wide (TWiLight Menu++: Screen Aspect Ratio); auto follows the console.
HelpLayout = Which button does what: classic, modern, buttons only (no stylus), or your own (BUTTONS).
HelpHand = Which hand holds the stylus: the touch screen's buttons go to the other side.
HelpCrouch = Crouch held down, or pressed once to crouch and again to stand.
HelpWalk = Walk held down, or pressed once to walk and again to run.
HelpJumpButton = What the jump button does: jump, or walk.
HelpTapJump = Two taps on the aim pad jump. Off, only the jump button does.
HelpTapUse = A quick tap on the aim pad is USE, in the modern layout. Off, USE's button does it.
HelpPadSide = What the pad's left and right do: step aside, or turn.
HelpPadUp = What the pad's up and down do: walk, or look up and down.
HelpAutoaim = The aim bends towards a monster near it, on EASY and MEDIUM, as the game's does.
HelpAimSpeed = How far the view turns across for a stroke of the stylus.
HelpPitchSpeed = How far the view turns up and down for a stroke of the stylus.
HelpInvert = The stylus moved down looks up.
HelpFirmness = How light a touch of the stylus is ignored.
HelpFirmPress = What pressing the stylus hard does, where the console can tell.
HelpBob = The view bobs with a walk and leans into a step aside, as the game's does.
HelpReadout = Numbers along the top of the touch screen: how fast, where, how much.
HelpPopups = An achievement earned is shown on the top screen as it is. Off, it is still earned.
HelpAutosave = The game saves at a map's save points and on arriving in a map. Off, only your own saves are made.
HelpHideMapName = The loading screen does not say which map is coming. A match's always says it.
HelpVibration = How hard a Rumble Pak in the GBA slot shakes when you are hit, shoot, land hard or the ground shakes.
HelpCheats = Lets the COMMAND line's cheats work. On, Cheater is earned and no other achievement is until it is off.
HelpKeyboard = Where the letters are on the touch keyboard: QWERTY or AZERTY.
HelpKey = Touch an action, then press its button. START: leave it as it was.
KeyboardQwerty = QWERTY
KeyboardAzerty = AZERTY
HelpFirmPressNoSense = This console does not say how hard the stylus presses: not used here.
# The box under the list, while a button is asked for, and before a row is touched.
PressItsButton = Press its button now. START: leave it as it was.
HelpSheet = Touch a setting to change it, or to read what it does; drag a slider. Slide the list, or touch < >, for the other settings. RESET: this page's as they were at first.
