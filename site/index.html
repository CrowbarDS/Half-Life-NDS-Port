Half-Lifefor Nintendo DS

The original campaign running natively on the DS and DSi, with local wireless multiplayer and Download Play.

No game files come with it. You bring your own copy of Half-Life, and the converter turns it into the files your console reads from its SD card.

Features

The whole game, at DS scale

The world

  • The game's maps, with their lighting, textures, water and decals, drawn by the DS's 3D hardware
  • Movement that plays like the original: walking, climbing, swimming, ladders

The game

  • Weapons, monsters and people, with the game's AI, scripted scenes, doors, lifts, trains and breakables
  • Three difficulties, the Hazard Course, and Half-Life: Uplink if your copy has it
  • Autosave, quick save, save slots and achievements

The console

  • Music and sound, with DSi-only extras where the extra memory allows
  • Multiplayer over local wireless, and Download Play for consoles with no card
  • Touch controls for either hand, with two button layouts

Getting started

Three steps to Black Mesa

  1. Download the converter

    One package for your computer. It has the ROM built in. Pick yours on the download page.

  2. Convert your own copy of Half-Life

    The converter finds your game and your SD card, and writes the game's data and the ROM to the card.

  3. Start the game on your console

    Launch half-life-nds-port.nds from your homebrew loader, such as TWiLight Menu++.

  • Hold WALK on the touch screen to walk (Half-Life runs by default), or set it to toggle.
  • Touch a weapon's slot to take it, or to reload the one in your hand. Touch RELOAD, JUMP, DUCK or USE for what they say.
  • Start, or MENU on the touch screen, opens the pause menu. Select, or the lamp, turns on the suit's lamp.
  • Stylus hand: left mirrors the touch screen and swaps the buttons over.
Multiplayer

Local wireless

On the title screen, choose MULTIPLAYER, then HOST GAME on one console and JOIN GAME on the others. The host presses START when everyone is in.

Each console needs the ROM and a card with the game converted, or it can get the game from the host by Download Play. Tested on Nintendo DSi and Nintendo 3DS, and through Download Play in both DS mode and DSi mode.

3DS

Widescreen

On a 3DS the game can fill the wider top screen at 16:10, seeing more to the sides at the same height.

  1. Make a widescreen TwlBg.cxi with TWPatch, save it as sd:/_nds/TWiLightMenu/TwlBg/Widescreen.cxi, and turn on external FIRMs and modules in Luma3DS.
  2. In TWiLight Menu++, press Y on the game and set Screen Aspect Ratio to 16:10.
  3. In the game, OPTIONS > GRAPHICS > Screen is auto by default, or set 4:3 or 16:10 yourself.
